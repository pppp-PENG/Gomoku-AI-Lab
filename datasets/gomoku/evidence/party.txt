      <article><span class="detail-label" data-i18n="formatLabel">A SHARED FORMAT</span><h2 data-i18n="formatTitle">Ready for your next idea.</h2><p data-i18n="formatText">SGF is an established format for board-game records, with a game identifier for Gomoku and Renju. We use SGF FF[4], GM[4], a 15 × 15 board and UTF-8 text, compressed with gzip.</p><p data-i18n="profileText">Our open profile identifies each ruleset and game mode, recorded captures, Elo ratings, timestamps and per-move or Fischer clocks. Use a reader that supports Gomoku; a Go-only reader may interpret the rules differently.</p><a class="text-link" href="format.html" data-i18n="readFormat">Read the format specification ↗</a></article>
